Signs you may have a leak

Hidden leaks rarely stay hidden for long if you know what to look for.

  • A sudden jump in your water billSame habits, higher cost.
  • Soft or warped spots in walls, ceilings or floorsWater is probably sitting underneath.
  • Musty or mildew smellsMoisture is building up where it shouldn’t.
  • The sound of running water when nothing is onA classic clue.
  • Low pressure, or hot water that runs out fastLeaks can reduce both.
  • Water stains or bubbling paintMoisture inside the wall often shows on the surface.

Quick answers about hidden leaks.

Turn off every tap and appliance, then check your water meter. If it keeps moving, water is escaping somewhere. Also watch for musty smells, warped surfaces and damp spots.

We start with where the water shows up and the most likely sources, then narrow it down. Sometimes we need access behind a wall or ceiling to confirm the source.
