Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about drain cleaning.

How do I know if I need professional drain cleaning?

If water drains slowly, backs up into other fixtures or the clog keeps returning, it’s time to call. Plunging can help briefly, but a repeat clog usually means the blockage is deeper.

Are chemical drain cleaners safe?

They can damage pipes, especially older ones, and often fail on deeper clogs. Mechanical clearing is usually the safer choice.

How long does it take to clear a clog?

It depends on where the clog is and how severe it is. We’ll give you an idea of the time once we see the problem.

Can you tell what caused the clog?

We look for the cause as we work and explain it, whether it’s grease, hair, debris or something deeper in the line.

What if the clog is in the main sewer line?

We handle main line blockages too. Tell us what you are seeing and we’ll advise on the next step.

What causes sewer backups

Knowing the cause is how you stop the next one.

  • Roots in the main lineThey enter old pipes and grow until little can pass.
  • Wipes, grease and debrisWhat goes down the drain doesn’t always go away.
  • Pipes that sag, crack or collapseA damaged section can stop the whole line.
  • Heavy rainOverloaded municipal sewers can push water back toward your home.
  • Earlier clogs that were never fully clearedA partial fix means the next backup is only a matter of time.

When the drain backs up, everything stops.

A sewer backup is a health and property risk, so treat it as urgent. Whether the cause is roots, grease, damaged pipe or a surge in the municipal sewer, Drain Squad Plumber clears the line and works to find the real cause so the fix lasts.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about sewer backups.

What should I do first during a sewer backup?

Stop using water: no flushing, laundry or running taps. If it is safe, shut off your home’s main water valve. Then call us.

Why did this happen?

Common causes are roots, grease buildup, damaged pipe and overloaded municipal sewers. We look for the cause so the repair lasts.

Can heavy rain cause a sewer backup?

Yes. When municipal sewers are overwhelmed, water can be pushed back toward homes. Ask us how to reduce the risk at your property.

Will insurance cover a sewer backup?

Coverage depends on your policy and often requires specific sewer backup coverage. Photograph the damage and keep records of the work done.

How can I prevent it in the future?

Keep wipes and grease out of the drains, and book drain cleaning at the first sign of a slowdown. Ask us what suits your home.

Signs you may have a leak

Hidden leaks rarely stay hidden for long if you know what to look for.

  • A sudden jump in your water billSame habits, higher cost.
  • Soft or warped spots in walls, ceilings or floorsWater is probably sitting underneath.
  • Musty or mildew smellsMoisture is building up where it shouldn’t.
  • The sound of running water when nothing is onA classic clue.
  • Low pressure, or hot water that runs out fastLeaks can reduce both.
  • Water stains or bubbling paintMoisture inside the wall often shows on the surface.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about hidden leaks.

How can I tell if I have a hidden leak?

Turn off every tap and appliance, then check your water meter. If it keeps moving, water is escaping somewhere. Also watch for musty smells, warped surfaces and damp spots.

How do plumbers find hidden leaks?

We start with where the water shows up and the most likely sources, then narrow it down. Sometimes we need access behind a wall or ceiling to confirm the source.

Is leak detection expensive?

It costs far less than water damage. We recommend detection only when it makes sense for your situation, and we explain the price first.

Can I find a leak myself?

You can check visible pipes, fixtures and appliances. For a hidden leak, professional help saves guesswork and unnecessary damage.

Can leak sensors help?

Smart leak sensors and automatic shut-off valves can warn you early, even when you are away. Ask us if prevention is your goal.
