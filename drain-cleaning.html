Clogs we clear

Most clogs get worse, not better. These are the ones we see most often.

  • Kitchen sinks that drain slowly or not at allGrease, food waste and buildup narrow the pipe over time.
  • Showers and tubs that fill while you washHair and soap scum collect and harden.
  • Gurgling toilets or water rising in other fixturesOften a sign of a clog further down a shared line.
  • Basement floor drains that back upThis can point to a blocked main line, so don’t wait.
  • Main sewer line blockagesRoots, damaged pipe or long-term buildup can stop the whole system.

Clear the clog. Restore the flow.

A slow drain is easy to ignore until it backs up. Drain Squad Plumber finds what is blocking the line and removes it, using professional equipment and proven techniques. We explain what we found and what to do so it doesn’t come back.
